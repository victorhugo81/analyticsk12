Defines the subject-area credit requirements shown on each student's Graduation tab. A course counts toward a subject area if its Department matches (and, optionally, its course name contains/excludes a keyword). Any course that matches no row below falls into whichever row is marked Catch-All.

Grade Span controls the Graduation Status dashboard's pacing: a subject typically taken in a single year (e.g. Algebra I in 9th grade) is expected fully complete by the end of that year, not spread evenly across all 4 years — so a freshman who's finished their actual 9th-grade coursework shows as On Track instead of being penalized for subjects (like CTE or 11th/12th-grade Social Science) that aren't due yet.

Each subject area can set a lower Continuation credit target for sites whose Graduation Track (set on the Site record) is Continuation — leave it blank to use the same amount as Standard.
